Formed by the survivors of [[Ancient Town]]. 
Settled in the outskirts of the northern parts of [[Rasemblé]]. It has tried its best to appear inconspiscious.

It did not take long until the [[Northern Rasemble caretaker]] realized who they really where. And agreed to keep it a secret in exchange of them staying solely in their land, among other things.

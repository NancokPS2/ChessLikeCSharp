Many years ago, a group of coffins was found buried in a sanctuary within a mountain's temple.
It contained 5 effigies that appear to represent real dead, adorned with all the decorations one would expect for kings.  
The real bodies or who the effigies where meant to represent was never found.

[[Dushe]] actually has a distant history of performing this ritual for people whose corpses could not be recovered. But this has fallen out of fashion since the last few centuries.

Entities born from the will of all living creatures.
Creatures with similar enough wishes contribute towards the same manifestation.

These entities see all the creatures it is shaped after as their creator, and someone they are destined to serve, which they do gleefully.

They do so by giving them the power to alter to world around them. The origin of creature-commended magic

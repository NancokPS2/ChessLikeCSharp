## Geography over time
Fittingly, it is the largest of the regions, with a mix of many cultures and governments. It is common for its inhabitants to refer to anything outside of it as the outer lands.

When the first maps where being drawn, most of the populace of this now-region where actually completely detached from each other.

The first map drafts that where preserved show that where there's [[Rasemblé]] now. There used to be a complete mix of dozens of smaller regions.

Seeing as these regions where so numerous and yet so small. Maps started to consider these as part of the [[Independent Sub-Regions]]. Some of the parts of [[Rasemblé]] remained as independent, while the ones that had more closer relations where given their current denomination.
With time, the members of the region grew closer together. It is debated if the decisions taking by cartographers had a big impact or not.

### Division
The division is quite loose and informal. Each "part", as they are called, is a loose representation of a settlement, town or city. Each of these parts is allowed to implement their own laws and such, as long as they respect the [[#Agreement of  Rasemblé]]

## Politics and government
### Agreement of Rasemblé
A set of rules that all parts of the region must respect to remain part of it.
Once a month, each part dispatches 1 to 3 of its citizens with a document carrying the opinion and wishes of its members to a central part of the region, in order to vote on various matters:
- Changes to the [[#Agreement of Rasemblé]]
- Changes in what geographic location constitutes each "part"
- Discussing how to enforce the agreement to those who have broke it.

#### Benefits
Some of the inherent and so far unchanged parts of the agreement include: 
- Pact of defense accross all parts of the region
- Regional army with troops coming from all parts of the region. Mostly in charge of solving issues stemming from violations of the agreement.
- Free and unsanctioned passage trough any of the parts

#### Consequences for breaking the agreement
Should the [[#Agreement of Rasemblé]] be broken. Neighboring parts are allowed to take immediate action as allowed by the agreement itself to stop the violations from affecting them. But the actual actions taken to stop it entirely must be discussed in the next meeting.
The consequences are according to the violation and at an attempt to stop it. Should it be deemed necessary, a part can be exhiled. Outer parts of the region can simply be detached and re-included in the [[Independent Sub-Regions]]. But in some cases (specially for those closer to its center) they may be evicted from the land entirely or forced to spread among other parts of the region.

## [[Wars]]
[[Rasemblé]] has been historically in conflict with [[Dushe]]. And has had some friction with neighboring [[Independent Sub-Regions]].

One of the main properties of [[Unit]]s is their "job" which defines their capabilities in combat.
*Originally referred to as a "soldier", the term fell out of favor with the emergence of more unique styles of combat or aid. Most of these master more than just regular combat, but incorporate some techniques from other sources magical*

### Jobs
- [[Phoenix Warrior]] (Melee with AoE and HP costs)
- [[Celestial Priest]] (Healer)
- [[Wisp Tamer]] (Summoner) 
- [[Vagrant]] (Jack of all trades)
- [[Artificer]] (Item specialist)
- [[Mimic Witch]] (Blue mage)

## Mechanics
Each [[Job Template]] has a list of [[Passive]]s and [[Ability]]s that they have access to. As well as slots for selecting which ones a [[Unit]] will be able to use within each [[Encounter]].
**They also apply modifiers to the [[Unit]]s stats that combine with the other jobs present in the [[Unit]].**
Slots:
- 10 Active slots
- 2 Passive slot
- 1 Forced passive slot (which holds the signature passive of the primary [[Job Template]])

### Leaders
Each faction in combat has a leader. The leader grants bonuses to its allies based on its job, only one leader can be active per faction at a time.
#### Swapping
Leadership can be changed before and during combat, and only once per turn of the current leader. So slow leaders will take longer to swap out.
#### Falling
If a leader falls in combat, the cooldown for [[#Swapping|swapping]] becomes twice that leader's [[Character Stats#Delay|turn delay]] and the current bonuses go down with the leader, serving as a penalization.

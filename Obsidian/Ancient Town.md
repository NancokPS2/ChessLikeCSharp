## Location
Located within the [[Independent Sub-Regions]] and home of [[Blood-borne Protector]]
A place barely standing after centuries. A victim of being in the crossfire of several wars.

During some of the older conflicts between [[Dushe]] and [[Rasemblé]]. When [[Rasemblé]] was still largely separated. This town stood between them, prosperous but small.  

## Story
### Shunned tradition
This town was unique for the fact that it housed practitioners of ancient necromantic spells to commune with the dead. Making them disliked and even feared at times.
Being unfortunate enough to be in the middle of several wars from multiple parties, their town was taken as a fort and forced to take sides more than once, and blamed of collaboration with either side even more.  

### War scars
Without any victors to clear them of the blame, and with time, the town started to be seen worse and worse by other populations, to the point of becoming a common enemy for no other reason than being the most "disliked" of the small parts of the [[Independent Sub-Regions]]. 
No one would lend aid anymore, even fewer would visit. And the few that did, did so in secret to avoid association, more often than not to take advantage of their services for large sums which they could barely reject.
With an increasing amount of towns and villages refusing to perform trade with the degrading town, things started to cascade until it fell into desperate times.  

### Last stand
One day, the town worn down and weakened, faced its last and final threat. A group of bandits simply decided to take advantage of what remained of the town, to which its citizens lost. The bandits attempted to get rid of any survivors to not raise their notoriety any further, and that was the last the town faced before left as no more than burnt ruins.   
The wider world only knows someone attacked the town, but it is not something many care about due to the general aversion to it. Its story was left as a rare mention in history books.

### Preventive measures
After the massacre that happened at the city. In a last, heart wrenching ditch effort. The remaining survivors who managed to flee returned to the city, and pooled togheter the corpses of all the victims to perform a ritual, and grant them the ability to protect anyone who remained, the [[Blood-borne Protector]]

### Their whereabouts
After the fall of [[Ancient Town]]. The remaining survivors and the [[Blood-borne Protector]] fled and kept away from everyone else, seccluding themselves in a remote part of the north and pretending to be a new settlement to other passerbies by the name of [[Ancient Town II]].
